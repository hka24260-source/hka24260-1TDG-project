Different Techniques

At our Art Club, we explore a variety of creative techniques that help us develop our artistic skills and express our ideas. From creating detailed sketches to experimenting with different colour combinations, each technique gives us a new way to create and explore art. Below are some of the techniques we learn and can practise in our Art Club.

In art club, you will learn how to create detailed sketches using different types of pencil and different shading techniques. You will practise drawing shapes, proportions and small details to create a realistic artwork, such as an eye example. You will also learn how to use different types of lines, shading and blending to add depth and make your drawings look more three-dimensional. These techniques are a great way to develop observation, creativity, and attention to detail while building confidence in drawing.

Secondly, in Art Club you will explore the colours by learning how to mix and combine different colours to create new shades and tones. You will learn how primary colours can be mixed to create secondary colours and experiment with lighter and darker tones. This activity will help you understand colour relationships and how different combinations can change the mood and appearance of a network. It is a fun way to experiment, be creative, and discover new possibilities with the colours.
